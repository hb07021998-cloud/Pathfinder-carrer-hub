- [ ] Test the official government/examination source directory on mobile.
- [ ] For every government exam, scholarship, eligibility, vacancy, fee, salary, date or rule shown, confirm the specific detail against the latest primary authority notice. Remove or qualify unsupported/outdated claims.
- [ ] Confirm the privacy policy URL is publicly accessible and accurately describes data practices.
- [ ] In Play Console, update the app's website/privacy policy links if needed.
- [ ] Generate a new signed AAB in PWABuilder using the original keystore.
- [ ] Ensure versionCode is higher than the previously uploaded bundle.
- [ ] Upload the AAB to the intended testing track.
- [ ] Review the Play Console policy status and respond to the Misleading Claims issue with a concise explanation of changes and source links.
