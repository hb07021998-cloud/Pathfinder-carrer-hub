# Google Play store listing draft

**App name:** Pathfinder – Career Hub

**Short description (max 80 characters):** Explore study options, careers, exams and personal learning roadmaps.

**Full description:**

Find your next education or career step with Pathfinder – Career Hub, a student-friendly guide to learning and career options in India. Explore choices after school, higher secondary, graduation and postgraduate study.

Features:
- Explore courses, career fields and job pathways.
- Compare education routes and possible next steps.
- Discover exam and recruitment preparation guidance.
- Build a personal career roadmap.
- Track milestones and saved progress on your device.
- Switch between light and dark themes.
- Use the guide offline after the app has loaded and cached its files.

Pathfinder provides general educational information only. It is not affiliated with any government body, exam board, college, employer or recruiter. Salaries, eligibility, admission rules, fees, dates and recruitment conditions can change. Always verify details with the relevant official source. The app does not guarantee admission, employment or selection.
