Pathfinder Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

Pathfinder – Career Hub is an educational information app. This policy describes the app as currently supplied in this package.

Information collected

The app does not require an account and does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to a Pathfinder server. Career choices, saved pathways, and progress are stored locally in the browser/app storage on the device. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in this package.

External links

The app may link to third-party websites, including official education, examination, recruitment, and other information sources. Those websites have their own privacy practices. Review their policies before sharing information with them.

Storage and deletion

Saved app data remains on the device. Use the app's “Clear my saved data” control to remove Pathfinder-specific saved information from this app's local storage. Uninstalling the app may also remove its local app data, depending on Android behaviour and backup settings.

Children

This app contains general education and career guidance. The publisher must accurately declare the intended age groups and comply with applicable Google Play Families and child privacy requirements if the app is directed to children. This policy is not a substitute for those declarations or legal advice.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app functionality changes. For privacy questions or error reports, contact hb07021998@gmail.com.

Educational disclaimer

Career, salary, admission, eligibility, and recruitment details are general guidance and may change. Verify details with the relevant official institution or recruiting authority.
