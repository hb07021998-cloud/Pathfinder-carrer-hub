Privacy Policy – Pathfinder Career Hub

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Pathfinder – Career Hub is an independent educational guidance app. This policy describes the app's current on-device functionality.

Information and storage

The app does not require an account. Choices such as saved jobs, learning progress, theme preferences and roadmap settings are stored locally in your browser or device storage. The app does not intentionally send this locally saved information to a Pathfinder server.

External links

When you choose to open an external website, email link or search link, that destination is governed by its own privacy policy and practices. Review the destination's policy before sharing information there.

Children and students

The app provides general education and career information. It does not ask users to submit personal details to create an account. Students should use external services with the guidance of a parent, guardian or trusted adult where appropriate.

Data deletion

You can use the app's “Clear my saved data” control to clear locally stored Pathfinder preferences and progress, or clear the app's site data in your browser/device settings.

Changes

This policy must be updated if the app adds accounts, analytics, advertising, payments, server-side storage or other data collection.
